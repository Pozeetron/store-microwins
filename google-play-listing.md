## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
Micro-Wins is a gentle diary of tiny wins.

When big goals feel impossible and “just start” sounds cruel, this app is for you. Here, crumbs count: a sip of water, getting out of bed, opening a window, allowing rest.

No punishing streaks. No red alerts. No productivity race.

WHAT’S INSIDE

• Quick presets — log a tiny action in one tap
• Custom micro-wins — write your own if needed
• Soft warmth — calm progress without pressure
• Care Garden — grows from today’s crumbs and a living streak
• Daily mood — a soft note: soft / foggy / warm
• Daily ritual — one tiny practice for right now
• Calendar & gentle goals — a guide, not a verdict
• Quiet milestones — small signs that you’re holding on
• 10 interface languages — follows your device or your choice

WHO IT’S FOR

• Burnout and fatigue from “do more”
• ADHD and days when even small things are hard
• Anyone who wants a soft way to notice self-care

PRIVACY

• Stored only on your device
• No account
• No cloud sync
• No ad trackers

Micro-Wins is not therapy and does not replace professional care. It’s a quiet place to note: “a little worked today — and that’s enough.”
```
